# Code of conduct

**English** | [Español](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.es.md)

We want a community where people can ask, make mistakes and contribute without feeling bad about it. This code applies to issues, PRs, discussions and any other project space.

## What we expect

- Treat people with respect, also when you disagree.
- Criticize code and ideas, not the people who wrote them.
- Assume good faith and give context before judging.
- Accept a review gracefully, and give one kindly.
- Write in English or Spanish; be patient with people who don't master the language.

## What is not tolerated

- Insults, harassment, threats or personal put-downs.
- Discriminatory comments about origin, gender, orientation, disability, age, religion or any other condition.
- Unwanted sexual attention, doxxing or publishing other people's private data.
- Insisting after a "no", or deliberately derailing a conversation (trolling, spam).

## What happens if it is broken

Maintainers may edit or delete comments, close threads, warn, temporarily ban or permanently remove, depending on severity. We will try to be proportionate and explain the decision.

## How to report

If you see or experience conduct that breaks this, report it by email to <kevinccharpentier@gmail.com> (a private channel to the maintainer). It will be handled discreetly and there will be no retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith.
